The systems

Each module is powerful on its own — together, they feel like a living platform.

Physical

PMPE

Perception Engine that shapes sensation — touch, temperature, and multisensory response.

  • Safety-first modulation
  • Wearable-friendly modules
  • Game engine integration
Physical

PSCS

Spatial Computing System that anchors UI and worlds with stable, believable presence.

  • World-locked interfaces
  • XR-ready pipeline
  • System-to-system orchestration
Physical

ILCS

Locomotion Control for natural movement signals — designed for comfort and control.

  • Balance & intent modeling
  • Low-latency response
  • Safety and calibration layers

PBES

Personality & Behavior Engine that turns NPCs into consistent, adaptive beings.

  • Emotion + decision layers
  • Memory & adaptation paths
  • Developer-controlled safety

VCS

Vocal Cognition System for identity, speech behavior, and contextual voice response.

  • Voice identity system
  • Context-aware responses
  • Integrates with PBES
