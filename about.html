About Lumenix

Lumenix is a systems-first technology foundation for building interactive worlds that respond naturally to humans — physically, spatially, and behaviorally.

What it is

A foundation, not a single product

Lumenix is not one device, engine, or experience. It is a collection of interoperable systems designed to work together — or independently — to support next-generation immersive applications.

Why it exists

From scripted content to responsive worlds

Most digital experiences are pre-authored and reactive in shallow ways. Lumenix focuses on response: how systems sense, interpret, and adapt to real human input over time.

Who it’s for

Creators, developers, and builders

Lumenix is built for studios, developers, researchers, and partners who want to create believable worlds, characters, and interactions — without sacrificing safety or control.

The Lumenix systems

Each system is modular and purpose-built. Together, they form a coherent platform.

Physical perception

PMPE — Perception Engine

PMPE focuses on controlled multisensory perception. It is designed to safely modulate sensations such as touch, temperature, and other physical feedback channels.

  • Safety-first design with strict control layers
  • Modular and wearable-oriented architecture
  • Designed for integration with game engines and simulations

PMPE does not bypass human limits — it works within them.

Spatial computing

PSCS — Spatial Computing System

PSCS handles spatial awareness, alignment, and structure. It ensures that interfaces, objects, and interactions feel grounded and stable in space.

  • World-locked and context-aware interfaces
  • Designed for XR, simulation, and hybrid environments
  • Acts as a coordination layer between systems
Movement & intent

ILCS — Inertial Locomotion Control System

ILCS interprets human movement and intent to enable comfortable, controlled navigation through digital spaces.

  • Focus on comfort, calibration, and user safety
  • Supports natural movement signals
  • Designed to reduce disorientation and fatigue
Digital behavior

PBES — Personality & Behavior Engine

PBES governs how digital characters think, decide, and adapt. It focuses on consistency, memory, and believable behavioral change over time.

  • Emotion and decision modeling
  • Long-term behavioral consistency
  • Developer-controlled boundaries and safety frameworks
Voice & communication

VCS — Vocal Cognition System

VCS handles voice identity, speech behavior, and contextual communication. It allows characters and systems to speak in ways that reflect personality and situation.

  • Voice identity and variation
  • Context-aware speech behavior
  • Designed to integrate with PBES

Who works with Lumenix?

Lumenix is actively shaped with input from:

  • Developers building immersive applications
  • Studios exploring next-generation interaction
  • Testers and early collaborators
  • Partners evaluating long-term platforms
