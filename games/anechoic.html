What it is

Core concept

Players face personalized fear instances: either push through to escape faster, or endure longer while the threat approaches.

  • Co-op tension and shared consequences
  • Replayable sessions through variation
  • Focus on atmosphere and pacing

Built to prove systems

This project exists to stress-test Lumenix systems in real production — especially behavior, voice, and immersion.

  • PBES: adaptive NPC behavior (controlled)
  • VCS: contextual vocal responses
  • Optional: PMPE/PSCS integrations later
