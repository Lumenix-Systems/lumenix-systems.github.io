What it is

A horror experience built around perception, silence, and repeating patterns.

Core concept

You enter an anechoic chamber as a “test subject” for a new system. The environment begins to distort in ways that make silence feel active — like it’s pushing back. You must navigate a looping structure, interpret what’s real, and find a way out.

  • Story-driven horror with a looping progression
  • Sensory uncertainty: what you perceive may be wrong
  • Escape-focused pacing with rising pressure

Core loop (simple)

This is the repeating “game rhythm” the player experiences:

  • Enter / wake inside the chamber
  • Explore and test what rules are currently “true”
  • Stay quiet (or manage sound) to avoid escalation
  • Find clues or a route that breaks the loop
  • Attempt escape → loop shifts → try again with new understanding

Pillars (what makes it unique)

Pillars are the few big promises the game keeps delivering on.

  • Silence as gameplay: quiet isn’t just atmosphere — it’s a mechanic and a threat.
  • Perception pressure: you’re never fully sure if your senses are helping or lying.
  • Looping discovery: each loop teaches something new, not just “restart and repeat.”
  • Escalation: the longer you’re trapped, the more the chamber asserts itself.

Lumenix relationship (public-safe)

Anechoic is built as a real-world proving ground for Lumenix-level thinking: clean systems, believable feedback, and controlled escalation — without needing special hardware to be playable.

  • Playable as a standard game first
  • Leaves room for deeper integration later (optional)
  • Focus on clarity, safety, and controlled intensity
