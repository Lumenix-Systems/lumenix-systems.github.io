What it is

A supermarket simulator where your systems matter — because customers actually behave like customers.

Core concept

Build and run a modern supermarket: plan layouts, stock shelves, price products, and manage flow. The challenge isn’t just “place items” — it’s adapting to customers who respond dynamically to your choices.

  • Full control over layout, stocking, and planograms
  • Customers create emergent problems (queues, demand spikes, chaos)
  • Systems-driven gameplay with lots of “small decisions” that matter

Core loop (simple)

The repeating rhythm you’ll be living in:

  • Design or edit planograms + store layout
  • Stock shelves and handle inventory flow
  • Customers shop, react, and generate issues
  • Resolve bottlenecks (restock, re-layout, repricing)
  • Improve systems and expand the store over time

Pillars (what makes it unique)

The big promises Megamarket aims to deliver:

  • Dynamic customers: behavior that tests your systems instead of following scripts.
  • Planogram gameplay: shelf decisions matter, with real constraints and tradeoffs.
  • Messy realism: imperfect stocking, imperfect flow, imperfect outcomes — in a fun way.
  • Scalable variety: tons of products without hand-authoring everything.

Lumenix relationship (public-safe)

Megamarket is a systems playground: it exists to validate large-scale simulation thinking and believable behavior under pressure — the same mindset that powers Lumenix.

  • Focus on data-driven content pipelines
  • Simulation and emergent behavior as “the fun”
  • Designed to scale without turning into content bloat
