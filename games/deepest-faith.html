What it is

A multiplayer tabletop horror mystery where investigation is a bargain — and the cost is real.

Core concept

Deepest Faith is an online multiplayer tabletop horror experience. Players investigate a mystery tied to the Watcher while a looming time pressure forces decisions. Clues and keys push the investigation forward, but every step can increase risk: vulnerability, exposure, and consequences that change the run.

  • Investigation with real tradeoffs
  • Time pressure that creates tension (not just speedrunning)
  • Progress can open doors — and open you up

Core loop (simple)

Here’s the repeated “rhythm” a session aims for:

  • Choose where to investigate (tabletop-style options / locations / leads)
  • Search, solve, or negotiate to gain a clue or key
  • Pay a cost to take progress (risk, exposure, resources, or time)
  • Trigger consequences: the Watcher reacts, the world shifts, new obstacles appear
  • Regroup and decide: push deeper or play safe
  • Race the timer to complete the mystery — or accept a “worse than losing” outcome

Pillars (what makes it unique)

The big promises Deepest Faith is built around:

  • Progress has a price: every major advancement comes with a meaningful tradeoff.
  • The Watcher responds: the mystery pushes back; you are not investigating a passive world.
  • Winning vs surviving: success isn’t always “best ending” — sometimes the safe choice is to back off.
  • Tabletop tension online: readable choices, shared suspense, and social pressure in multiplayer.
  • Escalation curve: the deeper you go, the less forgiving the game becomes.

Lumenix relationship (public-safe)

Deepest Faith is developed as a game first. It does not require Lumenix hardware or special systems to play. As Lumenix evolves, the project can optionally become a strong showcase of responsive behavior and interaction — but public development stays focused on the game itself.

  • No Lumenix system dependency (currently)
  • Designed to be expandable as the ecosystem grows
  • Collaboration/testing will shape what becomes possible
