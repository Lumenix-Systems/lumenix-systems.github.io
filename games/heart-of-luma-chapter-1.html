Overview

Heart of Luma — Chapter 1 introduces players to the world of Luma and the emotional threads that connect its people and places. Through exploration, conversation, and small discoveries, the player slowly uncovers hidden stories and learns how seemingly ordinary moments can shape something much bigger.

The first chapter focuses on atmosphere, character relationships, and the beginning of a larger narrative that will continue across multiple chapters. It is designed to be a calm but emotionally meaningful experience that introduces the themes and tone of the wider Luma series.

Core Loop

  • Explore the world and interact with its environments
  • Meet characters and uncover personal stories
  • Discover clues that slowly reveal the larger mystery
  • Make small choices that influence conversations and relationships

Design Pillars

  • Emotion-driven storytelling
  • Calm and immersive exploration
  • Meaningful character interactions
  • A world that rewards curiosity
